Your extension signs one fixed message; the signature becomes a DigiByte wallet that lives in this browser. The derived wallet belongs to the signing account — a different account (or a different wallet app) derives a different wallet.

You’ll sign the same message twice — the second signature proves your wallet signs deterministically.

Even if your wallet uses a hardware device, the derived DigiByte wallet is a hot key in this browser.
